Kyou 今日 — today's agenda & tasks

kyou (今日, "today" in japanese) is a little app i made because other task apps kept overwhelming me with giant calendar views and endless lists :(

it just tries to answer one question: what am i meant to be doing right now? it reads from your system calendar and reminders so everything stays in sync with your other devices ♡
